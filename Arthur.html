Arthur

concept artist • 2d artist • 3D modeler

As part of the Art team, I focused primarily on the game's 2D visual development. My responsibilities included creating concept art and visual guides for a wide range of assets, including the main character, enemies, ability masks, interactive statues, and the environments for the second and third levels. These concepts helped establish the artistic direction for many of the game's key elements.I also contributed to the creation of visual effects and particles, animating several of them and working closely with the programming and design teams to enhance the feel of the game and to improve the visual effects used in its mechanics.In addition, I produced 2D illustrations for various gameplay systems and mechanics, which were used in the game's tutorials as well as in promotional and marketing materials.Finally, I was responsible for the design of the logos for both the team and the game, from the initial concept stage through final vectorization.
