Clara Rodriguez

Lead artist • 2D artist • ui artist

As the Art Lead throughout this project, I was responsible for organizing and coordinating the art department. It has been an honor to work with such a talented and dedicated team, and I am really proud of everything we achieved together. The collaboration, creativity, and commitment of every team member played a key role in bringing the project to life. Aside from organizational responsibilities, my main task was designing the game's UI, including the health bar, stamina bar, pause menu, and main menu layout. I also created the tutorial illustrations, such as controller and keyboard button icons and basic illustrations. In addition, I was responsible for all artistic aspects related to the narrative, including the dialogue boxes, portraits of the two main characters, and the opening comic panels that provide context for the protagonist's story.
