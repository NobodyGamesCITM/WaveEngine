SON OF ITHACA

You are Telemachus, and your mission is to rescue your father from the depths of the underworld. Explore and obtain masks that will grant you the power of the gods. 

GAME PILLARS

Exploration

Explore a vast world with multiple secrets to be found. Open hidden chests and gain potions that will help you during your adventure.

Combat

Learn to fight using basic attack combos, or use the masks of the gods to gain new combat abilities, such as ranged attacks, dashes, etc.

Puzzles

Using the masks of the gods, you can solve puzzles that block your path. Solving puzzles is the key to make progress through the game.

THE WORLD

MASKS OF THE GODS

HERMES MASK

This mask allows the player to float for a limited time, being able to move through water.

A​POLLO MASK

This mask allows the player to throw light projectiles that deal damage and activate certain structures

A​RES MASK

This mask gives the player more strength, allowing them to push heavy boulders through the map.
