isaac ramirez

player programmer • vfx programmer

Throughout this project, I have been almost exclusively focused on creating a solid player controller for the game's protagonist, Telemachus. From implementing basic movement in the earliest alpha versions to programming all of his mechanics, visual effects, and improving the overall game feel as much as possible. My main responsibilities, in a very summarized way, have been: - Programming the player's core mechanics and states, ensuring they can adapt to design requirements and fit a 3D game of this style, both for controller and keyboard users. This includes movement, combat, orbiting mechanics, animation integration, cutscene logic, and more. - Developing all gameplay mechanics related to the game's three main masks. - Finally, enhancing the visual side of the experience through a wide range of particles, lighting effects, textures, camera shakes, combat feedback, and other polish features.
