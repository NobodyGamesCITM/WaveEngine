Ivan larez

3d modeler • 3D ANIMATOR

My main role was Environment Artist. For level 1, I helped model the terrain, do UVs, and texture them, creating all the textures in Substance Designer. I textured every model individually, adding details like footprints, paths, and burned areas for the statues. For level 2, I created the textures directly in Substance Designer, and I adapted them inside the engine. In addition, I made the first version of the Main Character's model and his idle animation. I also modeled the portal, and some props, like flowers, grass and trees. Finally, I decorated level 1 and level 2 in the engine, and placed all the terrain models.
