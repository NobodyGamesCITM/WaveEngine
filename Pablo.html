Pablo sanjose

camera programmer • puzzle programmer • vfx programmer

I took charge of the camera, VFX, puzzles, and save/load systems, ensuring engine stability across both levels. I implemented full Gamepad Support (inputs and custom rumble), programmed the foundational player state machine, and refactored the Lua module to support direct script-to-script cross-references without scene-instantiated GameObjects. I created a JSON save/load framework using getters and setters to serialize player/world progression, masks, perks, enemy, and puzzle states, while unifying level transitions into a clean, single-script manager.I completely rewrote the camera architecture with modular "Camera Zones" (pan, zoom, ortho/perspective transitions, fixed angles) and implemented an engine keyframe cinematic system for all major cutscenes. This system featured custom smoothing, pause menu DeltaTime optimization to freeze cameras correctly, contextual puzzle door triggers, and a 4-way multidirectional enemy lock-on mechanic with adaptive rotation and scaling target particles.Additionally, I developed a modular grid-based puzzle system (PuzzleManager/GridElements) and the core progression loop linking statue keys to portal materials in real-time. Finally, I expanded the particle engine that was created by me at the begining of the project with spritesheets and Lua bindings, developed environmental VFX (leaves, checkpoint sparks), assisted with player trails and Level 2 blockouts, wrote technical documentation, and resolved GameplayQA tickets.
