Sauc pellejero

UI/HUD DESIGNER • NARRATIVE DESIGNER • QA

My main contribution to the project as part of the design team was to the UI/HUD design and the narrative design.At the beginning, it was my job as narrative designer to think of possible plots, characters, and ways to connect them to the gameplay, doing research to create an experience in line with the theme. I focused on merging the different ideas into one that the overall group liked and was excited to work on.Then, my main task was designing the user interface (UI) and heads-up display (HUD) so that it adapted to all the mechanics of the game and was intuitive and visually consistent with the project's overall vision, including health bars, mask spaces, tutorial pop-ups, and dialogues. It was also my responsibility to design the different screens that form the game, ensuring a flow between them and avoiding visual inconsistencies.Whenever a new version was made, I performed quality assurance (QA) to find any bugs or parts that needed improvement by playing the game multiple times and conducting exhaustive runs.Finally, I designed the webpage for the game, maintaining communication with the other departments to ensure every aspect of the project was reflected on the site. I also worked on a significant portion of the documentation, especially the GDDs and TDDs related to my areas.
