Victor gonzalez

Enemy Designer • Gameplay Designer • qa

As a member of the Design Team, my role focused entirely on system and combat design, specializing in enemy conceptualization, encounter balance, and player statistics. At the start, I pitched ideas on adapting specific segments of the Odyssey and curated the list of potential enemies that would fit the game’s theme and narrative.My main responsibility was designing the behaviors, combat roles, and grouping of the enemies. I designed distinct archetypes to create diverse combat encounters: melee units (Skeletons), ranged threats (Sirens), and chargers (the Minogoat). I also focused heavily on encounter design, planning how these enemies would fight together in groups to create engaging combat scenarios. Furthermore, I decided that Achilles would be the final boss, designing his complete stats, phases, and attack patterns, while also managing the base statistics of the player.Beyond that, I performed extensive QA testing focused specifically on refining the combat gamefeel and giving feedback on attack impacts. Finally, I contributed substantially to the documentation including updating the GDD and TDD, and provided iterative feedback and ideas to other departments and Scrum groups.
