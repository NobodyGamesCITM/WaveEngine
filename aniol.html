ui programmer

During this project I was focused most of the time on doing UI and narrative.With the help of Noesis GUI, I developed the first iteration of some UI systems for our game; these include:A dialog system that helps on the task of providing a narrative for our game and puts context on the player for what the game is about. Following that same objective, I developed a vignette system to explain the origin of the story in a simpler and defined way.Another system is the tutorial pop-ups; these pop-ups guide the player through the first steps. It's a way of doing tutorials easily that doesn't stop the gameplay. For this system, I also developed a system that detects if you are using a controller or a keyboard; in that way, it helps you, whichever way you are playing the game.The last system I developed is the credits; they show all our team at the end and some concepts to show the dedication throughout this journey.
