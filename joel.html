joel vicente

enemy ai programmer

Throughout this project, I have contributed to multiple areas of development, always from my role as a programmer. My responsibilities evolved throughout the different stages of production, allowing me to work on engine systems, gameplay features, puzzle implementation, and enemy combat behavior. My main contributions : - Working on engine development alongside other programmers, implementing scripting functionality and several quality-of-life features to improve the development workflow. - Developing core gameplay systems, including the mask system, stamina management, checkpoints, and animation integration. - Acting as Scrum Master for the puzzle team, while also implementing the doors that gated puzzle areas and the mandatory combat. - Focusing on enemy-related combat systems during the final stages of development. In particular, I was responsible for the behavior of the skeleton enemies, including their AI logic, combat interactions, and overall integration into the game's combat experience.
